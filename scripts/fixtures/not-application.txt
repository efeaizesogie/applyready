Weekend recipe: smoky jollof rice for six

Blend four tomatoes, two red bell peppers, one scotch bonnet and one onion until smooth. Fry a sliced onion in oil until soft, add two tablespoons of tomato paste and cook for five minutes. Pour in the blended pepper mix and simmer for 25 minutes until reduced. Add stock, thyme, curry powder, bay leaves and salt. Stir in three cups of washed rice, cover with foil and the lid, and cook on low heat for 30 minutes. Let the bottom catch slightly for that smoky party flavour. Serve with fried plantain and chicken.
