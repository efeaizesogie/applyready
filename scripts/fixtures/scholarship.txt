Northbridge Global Futures Scholarship 2027 — Call for Applications

The Northbridge Foundation invites outstanding graduates from Africa, South Asia and Latin America to apply for fully funded Masters scholarships at partner universities in the UK and Canada for the 2027/28 academic year.

What the scholarship covers
- Full tuition fees
- Monthly living stipend of £1,450
- Return economy airfare
- Visa application fee and health surcharge

Eligibility
- Citizen of an eligible country and resident there at the time of application
- A first degree with a minimum Second Class Upper (2:1) or equivalent
- At least two years of full-time work experience after graduation by the time of application
- Have applied to at least one eligible Masters course at a partner university
- Commit to returning to your home country for a minimum of two years after your award ends
- English language requirement: IELTS Academic 6.5 overall (no band below 6.0), or an accepted equivalent

How to apply
Applications are submitted through the Northbridge online portal only. Email applications will not be considered.

Required documents
1. Completed online application form
2. Statement of purpose (maximum 1,000 words) explaining your leadership experience, why you chose your course, and how you will use the qualification to create change in your home country
3. Updated CV (maximum two pages)
4. Two reference letters, at least one academic. Referees must submit letters directly through the portal.
5. Official transcripts for all degrees
6. Copy of a valid passport data page
7. Proof of English proficiency (may be submitted after the deadline, by 30 April 2027)

Deadline: Applications close at 12:00 noon GMT on 5 December 2026. Late or incomplete applications will not be reviewed.
Shortlisted candidates will be invited for interviews in February 2027.
