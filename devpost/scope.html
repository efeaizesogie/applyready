ApplyReady

Paste an application announcement and get a plan that works backward from the deadline, then daily email nudges until every requirement is ticked off.

The unique kernel

Not a summariser. It's a deadline-driven checklist that chases you: every unticked item becomes a daily reminder with days remaining and a link back to where you stopped, and after you submit, it tells you when to follow up. A chat answer goes quiet once you close it; ApplyReady keeps reminding you until you submit and follow up.

The core loop

Who it's for

The person

A recent graduate juggling scholarships (BSc, Masters, PhD), admissions, jobs and grants, in any country.

What goes wrong today

They find opportunities but lose them in execution: essays late, documents missing, no plan, no follow-up. So they fall back on bulk-applying.

Why it matters

"Each application I send is worthy of being shortlisted."

Feel

Linear + Notion: clean, minimal, structured, calm. Calm and organised, not a nagging alarm.

Finish line (the demo)

Paste a real link → dashboard with ranked requirements, guidance, paced checklist → tick a couple → trigger "next day" → a real email lands naming exactly what's left → click it, back on the dashboard.
