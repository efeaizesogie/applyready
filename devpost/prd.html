Scope said "a checklist that chases you." Now we know exactly what that means: eight screens and states, three emails, and checks you can verify by looking.

Boundary

Building now
  • Landing page with paste box · staged scan progress · link fetch with blocked fallback
  • AI extraction: ranking, guidance, best-by dates
  • Dashboard with tickable checklist · My applications
  • Magic-link email · daily reminders + "send now" demo trigger
  • Mark submitted + one opt-in follow-up · dark/light theme
Deferred
  • WhatsApp reminders — verification time
  • Full accounts — magic link covers identity
  • Editing extracted details / custom items — not needed to prove the loop
  • Reminder time-of-day preferences
Non-goals
  • Writing essays for the user
  • Finding or recommending opportunities
  • Submitting applications on the user's behalf
